Teaching

Mathematics in context

Helping students connect mathematical structure with real systems, real data, and questions that matter.

Teaching philosophy

Active, inclusive, and problem-centered

Professor Kang creates an active learning environment that recognizes students’ different backgrounds, strengths, and goals. Mathematical ideas are developed through real-world questions, computational exploration, collaborative problem solving, and reflective communication.

Her teaching integrates research with education: students learn not only how to apply established methods, but also how to formulate assumptions, test models, interpret uncertainty, and communicate what mathematics reveals—and what it does not.

Learning priorities
  • Conceptual understanding before routine calculation
  • Models grounded in biological and social mechanisms
  • Computation as a partner to analysis
  • Clear mathematical writing and presentation
  • Research experiences across degree levels
Courses developed

Building the applied mathematics curriculum

Professor Kang helped develop ASU Polytechnic’s applied mathematics major and minor and created four courses that connect mathematics with complex systems, data, and biology.

Graduate

ABS 560 · Ecological Modeling

A research-centered course integrating ecological questions, mathematical formulation, analysis, computation, and interpretation.

Upper division

MAT 494 · Introduction to Complex Adaptive Systems

Emergence, adaptation, networks, feedback, and multiscale dynamics across natural and social systems.

Upper division

MAT 394 · Mathematical Methods and Data

Mathematical and computational methods for extracting structure, testing assumptions, and reasoning with data.

Upper division

MAT 350 · Techniques and Applications of Applied Mathematics

Core analytical and computational techniques organized around applications and mathematical modeling.

Foundational

MAT 265 · Calculus for Engineers I

Conceptual and computational foundations in differential and integral calculus, taught through active problem solving.

Research

Independent study, thesis & dissertation

Individualized research instruction across undergraduate, master’s, and doctoral programs in mathematics and life sciences.

Research-integrated learning

Students learn by building

Course and independent-study projects invite students to formulate models of ecological, biological, engineering, and social systems; analyze their behavior; and connect mathematical results back to the original question.

Program development

Applied mathematics at ASU Polytechnic

As a core applied mathematics faculty member, Professor Kang worked with colleagues to develop the Applied Mathematics major in 2016 and minor in 2017. The curriculum emphasizes interdisciplinary applications, data, modeling, and preparation for research or technical careers.
