Selected publications

A research portfolio spanning mathematical biology, complex adaptive systems, nonlinear dynamics, ecology, and human-autonomy interaction.

Publication record

140 peer-reviewed articles

The publications below emphasize recent and representative contributions. The full 2026 curriculum vitae documents 140 peer-reviewed articles and additional current manuscripts.

Professor Kang’s publication practice foregrounds student scholarship: students are typically listed first when they lead model development and analysis, while the mentor or senior author is generally listed last.
