Academic profile

Mathematics for adaptive, living systems

Professor Kang’s scholarship lies at the intersection of nonlinear dynamical systems, mathematical biology, and complex adaptive systems. Her research connects rigorous analysis with data-informed modeling to reveal how feedback, heterogeneity, and multiscale interactions generate collective behavior and critical transitions.

Her current program spans pollinator health and social insect organization, ecological and evolutionary dynamics, epidemiology and social behavior, and trust in human-autonomy teams. Across these domains, the central goal is consistent: translate complex mechanisms into interpretable mathematical structure and actionable insight.
